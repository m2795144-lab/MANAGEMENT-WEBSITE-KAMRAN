Guidelines for Authors

Prepare and submit your manuscript

The International Conference on Emerging Ideas on Sustainable Development Goals invites academicians, research scholars, industry practitioners, and members of professional bodies to submit original, unpublished research papers, conceptual frameworks, and empirical studies aligned with our conference themes. This premier event bridges academic theory and industry best practices to foster cross-disciplinary collaboration. We welcome your latest research, insights, and case studies to help shape the future of the field.

Manuscript Preparation & Formatting

  • Language: All submissions must be written in clear, concise English in a Microsoft Word format.
  • Length: Full-length papers should typically be between 5,000 and 8,000 words, including references, tables, and figures.
  • Structure: Papers should generally include an Abstract (150–250 words), Keywords (4–6), Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Results/Analysis, Discussion, Conclusion, and References.
  • Formatting: Authors must adhere strictly to the APA 7th edition style.

Submission Process

  • Online Portal: All papers must be submitted electronically through the designated conference submission platform.
  • Link: https://forms.gle/FxbQ9JqtHxUgb9VZ8
  • Submissions via email will not be accepted.
  • Blind Review: To facilitate a double-blind peer-review process, please ensure that the initial submission does not contain any author names, affiliations, or identifying information.
  • Title Page: A separate title page containing the paper title, author names, institutional affiliations, email addresses, and the corresponding track must be uploaded alongside the main manuscript.

Review Process and Evaluation Criteria

  • All submissions will undergo a double-blind peer-review process by members of the international review committee.
  • Papers will be evaluated based on originality, relevance to the conference theme and chosen track, methodological rigor, clarity of writing, and contribution to the field of sustainable development goals.

Plagiarism Policy & Ethics

  • The conference maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy toward plagiarism.
  • All submitted papers will be screened using professional plagiarism detection software.
  • A similarity index exceeding 15% (or any single source exceeding 2%) may result in immediate rejection without review.
  • Authors must guarantee that the work is entirely original and has not been published or submitted elsewhere concurrently.

Registration and Publication

  • All authors of an accepted paper must register for the conference. At least one of the registered authors must present the paper at the conference for it to be included in the conference proceedings.
  • Selected high-quality papers will be considered for publication in indexed conference proceedings or affiliated journals, subject to further review.
