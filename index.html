About the Conference

We are delighted to present the proceedings of this “International Conference on Emerging Ideas on SDGs”. Our world faces many challenges in the 21st century and the United Nations 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development aims to address these challenges and provide us with a plan to achieve peace, prosperity, people and planet. This conference provides an opportunity to connect ideas with scientific innovation to accelerate our progress towards achieving the seventeen Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

This conference has focussed on the importance of ‘Emerging ideas’. In order to meet the Sustainable Development Goals by the set deadline 2030, we need to innovate in new ways. We need disruptive technologies, we need innovative thinkers from different disciplines. The tracks and sub-tracks have been carefully created to ensure that the seventeen global goals work together.

About the School of Commerce and Management

‘School of Commerce and Management’ under Srinath University was established in 2021 as a private educational institute with an aim of providing quality education in the field of Commerce and Management. It offers various courses such as Ph.D, MBA, M.Com, BBA (Digital Marketing), BBA (Business Analytics), BBA (Banking & Insurance), BBA (International Business), BBA (Security Market), BBA (Accounts & Finance), B.COM (Hons), B.COM (Fintech), B.COM (Business Analytics), B.COM (Finance & Taxation) and B.COM (Tally Education).

The Department offers a blend of curriculum and extracurricular activities to enhance the learning and skill development in the students. These activities lead the students to improve their leadership and interpersonal skills along with bonding, interaction and development. The School has a dedicated placement cell which is responsible for enhancing required skill set and uplifting their employability factor. This cell regularly invites corporates for conducting placement activities and offering internship/training opportunities to the students.

About the Srinath University

Located in Jamshedpur, Jharkhand, Srinath University is a multidisciplinary institution committed to academic excellence, research, innovation and societal development. Established under the Srinath University Act, 2021, and recognised by the UGC under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956, the University operates under the aegis of Sandhya Shambhu Educational Trust.

The University offers diverse Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG) and Doctoral programmes through its Schools of Engineering, Commerce & Management, Science, Humanities & Social Sciences, Pharmacy, Education, Information Technology, Fine Arts, and Health & Allied Sciences.

With a strong focus on experiential learning, interdisciplinary education, industry–academia collaboration, research and innovation, Srinath University strives to nurture competent professionals, entrepreneurial thinkers and responsible leaders committed to sustainable development and nation-building.
